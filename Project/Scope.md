# Project Scope: Vaccination Scheduling


**1. Project Objective**
The main goal of this project is to create a web-based vaccination scheduling system that makes it easier for patients to book and manage their vaccine appointments. It will also help clinic staff keep track of vaccination records and identify patients who have missed or are due for a follow-up dose. This can help the clinic keep better track of patients and reduce missed doses.

**2. Target Users**
Patients: Can book, reschedule, or cancel appointments and view their vaccination history.
Clinic Staff: Can manage appointments, record vaccines that have been given, view patient records, and follow up with patients who are overdue.

**3. In-Scope Features**
Patient registration and login
Booking, rescheduling, and cancelling appointments
Staff dashboard for managing appointments
Recording administered vaccines
Viewing vaccination history
Automatically flagging patients who are overdue for a follow-up dose
Reminders for upcoming and missed appointments
Basic reports for clinic staff

**4. Out-of-Scope Features**
Payment or billing
Vaccine inventory management
Multiple clinic branches
Native mobile application

**5. Major Deliverables**
Working web application
Requirements and design documents
Database schema
Source code in GitHub
User guide
Test cases and testing report
Final report and presentation


***References***

The reminder and missed-dose features are based on research from the Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ), which explains that reminder and recall systems can help improve vaccination rates by reminding patients about upcoming appointments and following up after missed appointments.

Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality. Strategy 6R: Reminder Systems for Immunizations and Preventive Services. https://www.ahrq.gov/cahps/quality-improvement/improvement-guide/6-strategies-for-improving/health-promotion-education/strategy6r-reminder-systems.html
