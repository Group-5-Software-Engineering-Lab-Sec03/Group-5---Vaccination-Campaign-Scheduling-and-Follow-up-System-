# Core Processes Matrix

| **Process No. and Name** | **Input Data** | **Output Data** | **Rules** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 – Manage Accounts and Access | - Registration details of patient.<br>- Login credentials (patient, nurse, clinic staff)<br>- Request access role.<br>- User account record from D1. | - Account creation confirmation<br>- Account access/Login (patient, nurse, clinic staff)<br>- Role update confirmation<br>- New account record to D1 | Registration requires valid personal and contact information, and the email must be unique. Login succeeds only if the credentials match an account in D1. Each role (Patient, Nurse, Clinic Staff) gets access only to its permitted functions and privileges.. Only authorized clinic staffs can assign, change, or delete roles. |
| P2 – Manage Appointments and Waitlist | - Slot search details of patient.<br>- Booking, reschedule, cancel request (patient)<br>- Waitlist join/leave request (patient).<br>- Appointment and slot record from D2.<br>- Vaccine dose schedule from D4. | - Available slots list.<br>- Booking/cancellation confirmation.<br>- Waitlist status.<br>- Updated appointment record to D2.<br>- Missed visit record to D3. | A booking is only allowed for an open slot that matches the chosen vaccine and dose. When rescheduling, the new slot is booked first and then the old one is released. A cancelled slot is released right away and the waitlist is checked. A failure to appear also releases the slot but is saved separately from a cancellation. A patient can only join the waitlist if the slot is full. |
| P3 – Verify Eligibility and Administer Dose | - Dose details entered by nurse.<br>- Contraindication details (nurse).<br>- Deferral reason (nurse).<br>- Scheduled appointment from D2.<br>- Patient dose history and contraindications from D3.<br>- Dose intervals from D4. | - Eligibility result and warnings to nurse.<br>- Dose, contraindication, deferral and reaction records to D3.<br>- Appointment status update to D2.<br>- Next dose request to P5. | A dose can only be recorded after the eligibility check passes, which means no active contraindication and the minimum gap since the last dose is met. If the patient is not eligible or refuses, the nurse must pick a deferral reason and the appointment is marked "Deferred" and kept in the history. Each dose record saves the nurse ID, injection site, batch number and time. An adverse reaction is linked to the dose it happened after and has a severity of mild, moderate or severe. |
| P4 – Track Follow-up and Flag Overdue Doses | - Dose records from D3.<br>- Required doses and intervals from D4.<br>- Appointment record from D2.<br>- Flag review and override reason (clinic staff). | - Overdue flag to D3.<br>- Overdue patient list to clinic staff.<br>- Overdue reminder request to P5.<br>- Reminder cancellation to P5. | A patient is flagged when the next dose is past its interval for that vaccine and there is no completed or booked appointment for it. Clinic staff can override a false flag, but a reason must be entered and the reminder is cancelled. If the flag is correct, the reminder is still sent. |
| P5 – Send Reminders and Notifications | - Upcoming appointments from D2.<br>- Contact details of patient from D1.<br>- Overdue reminder request and reminder cancellation from P4.<br>- Open slot notice from P2.<br>- Next dose request from P3. | - Appointment reminder to patient.<br>- Overdue reminder to patient.<br>- Waitlist slot notification to patient.<br>- Next dose notice to patient. | Appointment reminders are sent at least 3 days before the appointment. Overdue reminders are sent for flagged patients unless staff overrode the flag. When a slot opens, only the next patient on the waitlist is notified. Nothing is sent if the patient has no valid contact details. |
| P6 – Provide Dashboard, History and Reports | - History request (patient).<br>- Dashboard filter and report request with date range (clinic staff).<br>- Appointment record from D2.<br>- Dose, reaction and flag data from D3.<br>- Patient details from D1. | - Vaccination history to patient.<br>- Daily appointments dashboard to clinic staff.<br>- Clinic summary report to clinic staff. | Patients can only see their own history, including past and upcoming doses and any reactions. The dashboard shows the day's appointments and can be filtered by time and vaccine type. Reports cover doses given, appointments booked and overdue patients for the date range chosen. |
