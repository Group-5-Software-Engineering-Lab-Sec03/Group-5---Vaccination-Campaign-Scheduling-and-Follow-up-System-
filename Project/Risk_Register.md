# Risk Register - Vaccination Campaign Scheduling and Follow-up System

For Exercise 3 - Lab 2. The risk register clarifies potential risks to the deployment and delivery of the vaccination campaign scheduling and follow-up system, intended for general vaccination clinics.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation/Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Requirements differ across clinics or vaccination types, as different dosing schedules with different intervals between doses can lead to unclear or conflicting system requirements. | Since the system is focused on targeting general vaccination campaigns rather than one known clinic, the workflows and scheduling rules are not standardized across all use cases | Medium | High | Instead of setting a fixed rule for one vaccine only, we will define a flexible dosing interval model and document our assumptions clearly in the Scope document. Before finalizing this model, we will validate it against at least one real clinic vaccination schedule to confirm it holds up in practice. |
| R2 | Sensitive health records and vaccination data are exposed, leaked, or accessed without authorization | Weak access control and security, storing personal health data without proper encryption or security software, or lack of role-based permissions between patient and staff views, allowing any staff to have top-level (admin) access | Low | High | Restrict record access by role (patients see only their own record, staff see records for their clinic and assigned patients only, designers and admins have access to maintain the software), avoid storing unnecessary personal data, and require authentication for all data inputs and endpoints |
| R3 | The automatic flagging system for missed follow-up doses produces incorrect results such as false positives and false negatives | Ambiguous business rules (rules and regulations) for what counts as a "missed" dose across different vaccine types, or bugs in date and schedule comparison logic | Medium | Medium | Define and document exact flagging rules early per vaccine type (days overdue before flagging), write unit tests against sample appointment data, validate flagged cases with a test user group during development, and schedule planned outages for maintenance activities that are short and fast |
| R4 | Team member unavailable or unable to complete assigned tasks on time | Overlapping exam schedules, coursework from other classes, or personal conflicts during the semester | Medium | Medium | Assign tasks early with buffer time before deadlines, maintain a shared task tracker, and have one backup member per major component so no single person is a bottleneck that halts operation |
| R5 | Integration issues between the booking module, record-tracking module, and flagging module | Modules developed independently by different members without an agreed data structure or shared repository conventions | Medium | Medium | Agree on a common data schema or structure (e.g appointment and record format) before individual development starts, integrate in an incremental order with a foundation, and test integration points early |
| R6 | Low adoption or engagement from patients and staff during testing and feedback stages | Users are busy, unfamiliar with the system, or reluctant to test an unfinished tool, especially without the tool already existing in a real clinic | Medium | Medium | Keep test sessions short and scheduled in advance, offer a straightforward demo and prototype rather than asking users to explore unguided, and gather feedback through short structured forms |
| R7 | Project falls behind schedule due to underestimated complexity of a feature (e.g supporting multiple vaccine types and schedules instead of one fixed schedule) | Broadening scope from a single, fixed vaccination program to general vaccination campaigns increases the complexity of design and testing | High | High | Start with support for one or two vaccine schedules as the working core, work our way to apply it flexibly as a stretch goal, and reassess to tweak the scope early if a milestone is missed rather than waiting until the deadline |
