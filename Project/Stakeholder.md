# Stakeholder Analysis - Vaccination Campaign Scheduling and Follow-up System

For Exercise 4 - Lab 2. This table identifies the major stakeholders of the vaccination campaign scheduling and follow-up system, their roles, interests, influence, and how the system affects them.

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest/Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patients | Book and manage their vaccination appointments | Easy booking and reminders. | Their feedback helps improve the system. | Makes booking and tracking vaccines easier. |
| Clinic Staff | Manage appointments and patient records. | Easy way to manage patients and doses. | Their needs affect the system design. | Reduces manual work and helps track missed doses. |
| Clinic Management | Oversee the vaccination process. | Accurate records and simple reports. | Helps decide what the system should include. | Gives them a better view of clinic activities. |
| System Administrator / IT | Manages user roles, access permissions, and system security/maintenance. | Reliable, secure system with clear role-based access and minimal downtime. | Their access-control decisions directly shape how patient vs. staff permissions are enforced in the system. | Adds responsibility for maintaining data security and managing user accounts. |
