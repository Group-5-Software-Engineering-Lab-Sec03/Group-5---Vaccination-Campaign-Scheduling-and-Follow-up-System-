The architecture has 5 components arranged in 3 layers. Layer 1: CO1 Access Management and CO4 Notification and Reminders. Layer 2: CO2 Appointment Scheduling. Layer 3: CO3 Clinical Care and CO5 Follow-up and Reporting. A component only uses interfaces of components in a lower layer. The Web Client and the external services (Relational Database, Email / SMS Gateway) sit outside the layers.

| ID | Component Name | Responsibilities | Supported Functionality (Lab 3) | Provided Interfaces | Required Interfaces |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CO1 | Access Management | Manages the accounts of patients, nurses and clinic staff: registration, login and logout, and the patient profile. Stores roles and their permissions, lets authorized clinic staff assign or revoke roles, and limits what each user can see or do. Other components call it to check a user's permission and to read a patient's history. | FR-06 (register an account), FR-10 (view vaccination history), FR-19 and FR-20 (assign roles and role-based access), NFR-17 (role-based access) | IAuthentication, IPatientAccess, IRoleManagement | IPersistence (Relational Database) |
| CO2 | Appointment Scheduling | Manages vaccine catalog metadata (total doses, intervals, boosters), checks and updates appointment slot capacity, handles appointment booking, rescheduling, and cancellation, and manages ordered waitlists to notify the next patient when a slot opens. | FR-03 (vaccine reference data), FR-04 (cancel an appointment), FR-05 (waitlist), FR-07 (search slots), FR-08 (book), FR-09 (reschedule) | ISlotSearchAndBooking, IAppointmentManagement, IWaitlistManagement, IAppointmentStatus, IVaccineSchedule | IRoleManagement (CO1), INotificationService (CO4), IReminderScheduling (CO4), IPersistence (Relational Database) |
| CO3 | Clinical Care | Checks that a patient is eligible for a dose (no active contraindication and the minimum interval since the last dose is met) before a dose can be given. Records the administered dose with batch number, injection site and time, and updates the patient's history. Records contraindications, deferrals (marking the appointment as deferred) and adverse reactions linked to the dose. | FR-02 (record a dose), FR-11 (verify eligibility), FR-12 (complete the appointment), FR-13 (contraindications), FR-14 (defer a dose), FR-15 (adverse reactions) | IEligibilityCheck, IDoseRecording, IClinicalRecords | IVaccineSchedule (CO2), IAppointmentStatus (CO2), IPatientAccess (CO1), IReminderScheduling (CO4), IPersistence (Relational Database) |
| CO4 | Notification and Reminders | Sends the notifications such as the booking confirmation and the slot-available message to a waitlisted patient, and retries a delivery that fails. Schedules the appointment reminder a set number of days before the visit and the overdue-dose reminder, and cancels a reminder when it is no longer needed, for example when staff override an overdue flag. | FR-05 (notify the waitlisted patient), FR-08 (booking confirmation), FR-16 (reminder 3 days before the visit), NFR-18 (reminder delivery and retry) | INotificationService, IReminderScheduling, IReminderControl | IMessageGateway (Email / SMS Gateway) |
| CO5 | Follow-up and Reporting | Finds patients whose next dose is overdue by using the vaccine dose intervals, and stores them as overdue flags. Lets clinic staff review a flag and override it with a recorded reason, which cancels the pending overdue reminder. Resolves flags when the missed dose is given. Produces the daily appointment dashboard and the summary report of doses, bookings and overdue patients for clinic staff. | FR-01 (review and override an overdue flag), FR-17 (summary report), FR-18 (daily dashboard) | IOverdueFlagManagement, IDashboardView, IClinicReporting | IVaccineSchedule (CO2), IAppointmentStatus (CO2), IPatientAccess (CO1), IReminderControl (CO4) |
